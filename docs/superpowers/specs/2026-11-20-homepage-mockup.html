I am a Ph.D. student in Computer Science at South Dakota State University, where I work with Dr. Ruyi Lian. My research is in geometric computer vision. I develop neural solvers for camera pose estimation that hold up under high outlier ratios and image noise, and I build benchmarks that test how well visual foundation models understand pose and 3D structure.

Before my Ph.D., I earned a B.Sc. in Information and Communication Technology from Islamic University, Bangladesh, and worked as a research assistant at the BUBT Research and Innovation Center in Dhaka. That earlier work spans medical image analysis, cybersecurity, and natural language processing, with papers in EURASIP Journal on Information Security, Array (Elsevier), Bioinformatics and Biology Insights, and Big Data and Cognitive Computing.

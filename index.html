About Me

I am a first-year Ph.D. student in Computer Science at South Dakota State University, working in the 3D Perception and Scientific Imaging Laboratory under Dr. Ruyi Lian. My current research centers on neural solvers for camera pose estimation, outlier-resilient 3D reconstruction, and benchmarks that probe how well modern vision foundation models actually understand geometry and pose.

This work is grounded in a publication record (9 publications, 298 citations, h-index 5, i10-index 4 on Google Scholar) spanning computer vision, medical image analysis, and cybersecurity, including work published in EURASIP Journal on Information Security, Array (Elsevier), Bioinformatics and Biology Insights, and Big Data and Cognitive Computing. I also serve the community as an ad hoc reviewer for PLOS ONE.

Research Interests: Computer Vision · 3D Reconstruction · Camera Pose Estimation · Multi-view Geometry · Geometric Deep Learning · Neural Solvers · Foundation Models for Vision · Medical Image Analysis · Explainable Machine Learning
